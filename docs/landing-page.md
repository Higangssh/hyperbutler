# Website

The website, https://hyperbutler.dev, lives in its own repository,
[Higangssh/hyperbutler-site](https://github.com/Higangssh/hyperbutler-site), and is served
by Cloudflare Pages from its `public/` folder.

Its numbers come from this repository: the README's "Current status",
[install.md](install.md) and [compare-docker-desktop.md](compare-docker-desktop.md). When
one of those changes, update the site too.
